# ADR-0000: Short decision title in imperative mood

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** Name or role
- **Supersedes:** none

## Context

State the forces at play. Include constraints that cannot be changed by this
project: platform rules, immutable published artifacts, licenses, and fixed
dependency versions. Keep this section factual and neutral; do not argue for
the outcome here.

## Decision

One paragraph in active voice: "We will ...". Then list the specifics later
sections are expected to cite by name: coordinates, versions, package names,
thread rules, and wire formats. A decision with open branches is not accepted.

## Consequences

### Positive

- What becomes easier, cheaper, or safer.

### Negative

- What becomes harder, more expensive, or impossible.
- Which consumers are excluded.

### Neutral

- Follow-up work that is implied but not part of this decision.
